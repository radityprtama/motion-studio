# Cinematic visual language

Use near-black navy, steel blue, and a small amber light source. Leave large quiet regions around the subject. Procedural silhouettes, a single lit orb, and sparse particles can establish scale or depth. Grain and vignette are subtle finishing, not the subject.

Animate with slow reveals and small camera changes when they direct attention. Show a count by making the whole group legible before highlighting a subset. The [crowd statistic](../../examples/cinematic/crowd_statistic.py) and [atmospheric title](../../examples/cinematic/atmospheric_title.py) are reference treatments. The style is restrained editorial imagery, not a neon dashboard.

Tokens live in `motion.style` under `cinematic`. All random details have explicit seeds.
